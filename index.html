OPERATIONAL PROTOCOL

From Mandi to Bill of Lading in six controlled stages.

We maintain physical presence at every handoff — origination mandi, processing mill, testing lab, container freight station and JNPT terminal.

01 PROCUREMENT

Origination & APMC Procurement

Direct sourcing from APMC-registered mandis across Maharashtra and Karnataka, cutting out speculative middlemen for transparent farm-gate pricing.

02 PROCESSING

Mechanical Grading & Sortex

Multi-deck mechanical screening, gravity de-stoning, and dual-camera optical Sortex color grading achieving up to 99.7% export purity.

03 ASSAY

Certified Laboratory Assay

Moisture titration via vacuum oven, pesticide residue screen, aflatoxin testing and protein assay with batch certificate.

04 PACKAGING

Export Packaging & Palletization

Packed in virgin food-grade PP bags (25/50kg) with optional PE liner, fumigated wooden pallets, and desiccants.

05 CUSTOMS

CFS Stuffing & Customs Clearance

Factory or CFS container stuffing under surveyor supervision, electronic ICEGATE filing, and Phytosanitary Certificate.

06 TERMINAL

JNPT Terminal Gate-In & Ocean Freight

Direct terminal gate-in at Nhava Sheva (INNSA1), vessel loading onto Tier-1 carriers, and prompt B/L dispatch.
